# Milestone 7: Business, design notes

**Date:** 2026-11-07. Modules: `multi-store`, `purchasing`. Reports, billing and plan limits apply to every tenant.

| Topic | Decision |
|---|---|
| Multi-store | Without the module a tenant has one store. `StorePrice(store, product)` overrides the catalog price at that store's tills: the API prices sales with it, down-sync sends the store's price, and offline verification checks against it (a change bumps the product's sync version). `UserStore` limits who appears on a store's PIN pad; staff with no assignment work everywhere. |
| Purchasing | Suppliers and purchase orders (`PO-000001`, per-tenant counters): DRAFT → ORDERED → PARTIAL → RECEIVED (or CANCELLED before anything arrives). Receiving adds stock at the order's store (`RECEIVE` movements referencing the PO), creates or tops up lots for lot-tracked products (lot number and expiry required), and sets the product's cost to the price paid. Over-receiving is refused. |
| Costs and margins | `Product.costPrice` (last purchase cost) is copied to each sale line (`unitCost`) when sold, so margins stay right after costs change. |
| Transfers | `TR-000001`: stock leaves the sender on send (`TRANSFER_OUT`, lots first-expiring-first), arrives on receive (`TRANSFER_IN`, lots recreated with the same numbers and dates). What arrived may be less than what was sent; the difference is recorded on the line and in the audit log and never reaches the destination's stock. Cancelling in transit returns stock and lots to the sender. |
| Insights | Revenue, cost and margin by product, category, store, staff or hour of day (tenant time zone); stock value at cost per store; low stock against `reorderPoint`; sale lines as CSV (amounts in major units for the tenant's currency). |
| Plans and billing | `Plan` gains a monthly price, currency, product limit and grace days; product creation enforces `maxProducts` alongside stores, devices and users. A daily job (02:00 Colombo) issues one invoice per paying tenant per calendar month, due a week in, and suspends tenants still unpaid `graceDays` after the due date (`suspendedReason = 'billing'`). A super admin records payments (bank transfer and the like); paying lifts a billing suspension once nothing is overdue. Plan changes that would leave a tenant over a limit are refused. Integrated payment providers remain out of scope, as the platform spec says. Owners see their plan, usage and invoices; super admins manage plans and invoices in the console. |
