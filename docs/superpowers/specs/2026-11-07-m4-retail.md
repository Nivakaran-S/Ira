# Milestone 4: Retail modules, design notes

**Date:** 2026-11-07. Every feature here is behind its module (`variants`, `barcodes`, `promotions`, `customers`, `loyalty`); the API refuses with `MODULE_DISABLED` and the apps hide what is off.

| Topic | Decision |
|---|---|
| Variants | A variant is a full product row with `parentId`, so price, SKU, stock, tax and barcodes per variant need nothing new in sales, stock or sync. The family (`hasVariants`) is not sold; the till opens a variant picker. |
| Barcodes | `Barcode(tenantId, code)` unique per tenant, many per product. Tills get them in down-sync (a product in the delta carries its full list, so removed codes disappear). Keyboard-wedge scanners are detected by key timing (under 60 ms between keys, ending with Enter); the search box also accepts codes and SKUs. |
| Customers | Phone and email AES-GCM encrypted; `phoneIdx`/`emailIdx` are HMAC blind indexes (key derived from the PII key with HKDF, input includes the tenant id) for exact lookup. Phones are normalised (local 0 → country code). Tills see name, last four digits and balances only. The audit log records which fields changed, not the values. |
| Promotions | Pure engine in `shared/src/promotions.ts`: percent off, amount off per unit, buy X get Y (whole units, cheapest in each group), spend threshold. One item promotion per line (buy-X-get-Y first, then the best of the rest), one spend promotion per sale. Promotions become `promo` discounts in the pricing engine; only `manual` discounts count toward manager-approval thresholds. |
| Offline promotions | The offline op carries what the till applied. The server re-applies its promotions at the sale time and compares, unless any promotion changed after the till's catalog version, in which case the till's version stands. Mismatches are quarantined (`PROMO_MISMATCH`). |
| Loyalty and store credit | Append-only ledgers (`LoyaltyLedger` in points, `StoreCreditLedger` in minor units), balance = sum, writers take a per-customer advisory lock. Points are earned on what was paid other than with points or credit; redeemed in whole points (`pointValue`, `minRedeemPoints`). Void restores redemptions and reverses points earned; refunds reverse earned points pro rata; refunds can go to store credit for sales with a customer. Online only. |
| Receipts | Promotions are listed by name, separately from manual discounts; customer name and points earned (with balance) are printed. |
